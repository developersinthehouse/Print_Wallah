# Design & UI/UX Guidelines: Stream 2 (Dashboards & Admin Panels)
**Brand Identity:** DEVELOPERS

## Visual Theme & Color Palette
* **Aesthetic:** Professional, data-dense, secure, and authoritative SaaS dashboard style.
* **Colors:**
  * Brand Accent: Indigo (`bg-indigo-600`, `#4f46e5`)
  * Background Surface: Slate Light (`bg-slate-50`, `#f8fafc`)
  * Cards & Containers: Pure White (`bg-white`) with clean borders (`border-slate-200`) and soft shadows (`shadow-sm`).
  * Status Indicators: 
    * Active / Healthy: Emerald Green (`bg-emerald-100 text-emerald-800`)
    * Expired / Locked: Crimson Red (`bg-rose-100 text-rose-800`)

## Layout Specifications (Desktop & Responsive Admin)
* **Screen Focus:** Optimized for desktop and tablet screens used by shopkeepers and administrators.
* **Visual Hierarchy:**
  1. Top navigation bar with DEVELOPERS branding and active shop/admin profile details.
  2. Stat summary cards (Total Prints Today, Revenue, Subscription Status & Days Remaining).
  3. Actionable data tables for managing rates, viewing job logs, or reviewing registered shop lists.
  4. Prominent warning banners and locked overlay states for accounts with expired subscriptions.
